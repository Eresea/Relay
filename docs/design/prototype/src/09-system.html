The four surfaces
Omnibar · Ctrl+Space · do, ask, find, run. Context chips say where you are. A preview pane says what it costs before Enter. Pill and stack · always one line; expands with Ctrl+. · asks only when blocked on you. Deck · the one window. Rail of modules, tabs that split, an inspector that follows selection, Flow in any pane. Phone · Today and the Omnibar, nothing else. Approvals travel; the PC stays the place work happens.
Rules that bite
Nothing loads until opened. A module that is off costs 0 MB and no startup time. The rail shows what is installed, not what is running. Say what it costs. Time, files touched, reversibility, tokens. Numbers in mono, specific or Unavailable. Agents are tenants. Same rows, same tiles, same approvals as a person. Their permissions are visible on every surface they appear on.
